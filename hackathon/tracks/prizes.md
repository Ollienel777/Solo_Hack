# Hack the North 2026 — prizes and tracks

Transcribed from the Devpost prize list (`devpost-prizes.pdf`, saved
2026-09-18, covering Devpost pages 4–11 of 16). The PDF is the source of
record; this file exists so that agents can read the tracks without paying for
page images. **Pages 1–3 and 12–16 of the Devpost page are not in the PDF**, so
dates, location, eligibility and part of the judge list are missing here.

## The event

- **Format:** open-ended, 36 hours. "Anything you want."
- **What to submit:**
  - a project built during the hackathon
  - a link to the source code, including all design assets created at the event
  - the badge ID of every team member, exactly as it appears under the QR code
    on their badge
  - **every sponsor prize you want to be considered for, selected before
    2:00 PM EDT on Saturday**
  - a demo video: optional but recommended
- **What judges look for:** originality, user experience, technical complexity,
  and WOW factor. **The judging pitch should be a live demo, not a slide deck
  or a product pitch.**
- 36 non-cash prizes.

## Main award

**Hack the North 2026: Finalists — 12 winners.** No first, second or third
place; top projects become finalists. "We love projects that are creative and
surprise us: original ideas, clever technical work, and creative experiences
that make people excited about what's possible." Playful, experimental,
wonderfully weird, or useful all qualify; no business plan required. Past
finalists: museum.hackthenorth.com. The finalist prizes are a surprise.

## Sponsor prizes

### OpenAI: API Prizes — 3 winners
Build something ambitious with the OpenAI API, with Codex as a development
teammate. Assessed on: what you built with the OpenAI API (how creatively and
effectively it powers the experience), and how Codex helped you build it (how
meaningfully it supported planning, implementation, testing, debugging or
iteration). In the demo: show the working product, explain how the OpenAI API
is used, and give one concrete way Codex improved the process or outcome.
Prizes: 1st lunch or dinner with OpenAI recruiters and engineers + ChatGPT Pro
+ swag; 2nd ChatGPT Pro + swag; 3rd backpack and T-shirt.

### RBC: Signal in the Noise — 1 winner
Financial research arrives as sprawling reports, transcripts and filings, too
large for any single context window. Design a custom agent/harness that ingests
massive real-world research data **via an MCP tool** and answers precise,
citable questions accurately, even when the corpus is noisy or contradictory.
Phase 1: master the known dataset — ingest, structure, query, proving accuracy,
precision and auditability at every step. Phase 2: **on judging day a
completely new industry dataset appears live**, and the harness must adapt
immediately; no pre-built solutions. Rewards smart retrieval, reliable
grounding and honest handling of ambiguity.

### Shopify: Hack Shopping with AI — 1 winner
Experiment where AI meets commerce. "Build something that makes us say 'wait,

tools. Optional: Shopify API, Remix, Polaris design system. Theme ideas (not
requirements): AI-powered merchant superpowers; commerce intelligence and data
insights; workflow automation; LLM applications in commerce; customer
experience enhancements. Judging: technical excellence (sophisticated,
appropriate use of AI with quality execution); impact potential (meaningfully
improves merchant operations or customer experience); innovation factor
(unexpected approaches). Prize: Shop Cash per person.
